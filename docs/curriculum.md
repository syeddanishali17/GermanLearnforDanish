# Curriculum: beginner patterns and the 12-week speaking sequence

Standard German first. Austrian/Vienna usage is a labelled layer. Every week ends with something Danish can say to a real person.

## Pattern codes and prerequisites

| Code | Pattern | Slot diagram | Prerequisites | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Verb in position 2 | `[Subject] [Verb] [rest]` · `Ich lerne Deutsch.` | none | `Ich Deutsch lerne.` |
| P02 | `sein` + identity/place | `Ich bin Student.` `Ich bin in Wien.` | P01 | `Ich bin ein Student` → `Ich bin Student` (no article for jobs/roles) |
| P03 | `haben` | `Ich habe eine Frage.` | P01 | `Ich bin eine Frage` |
| P04 | Yes/no question: verb first | `[Verb] [Subject] [rest]?` · `Ist hier frei?` | P01 | `Hier ist frei?` (rising tone only, informal) |
| P05 | W-question: W-word + verb | `[W-word] [Verb] [Subject]?` · `Wo ist die Mensa?` | P01, P04 | `Wo die Mensa ist?` |
| P06 | Regular verbs, Präsens | ich -e · du -st · er/sie -t · wir/Sie -en | P01 | `du lerne` |
| P07 | Gender in chunks | ▲ der · ● die · ■ das, learned with the noun | none | guessing gender from English |
| P08 | `ein/eine/kein` | `Ich habe kein Ticket.` | P07 | `nicht ein Ticket` |
| P09 | Akkusativ for objects | ▲ den/einen · others unchanged | P07, P08 | `Ich möchte ein Kaffee` → `einen Kaffee` |
| P10 | `möchte` / `hätte gern` + noun | `Ich hätte gern einen Kaffee.` | P09 | `Ich möchte einen Kaffee haben` (correct but long) |
| P11 | Modal + infinitive at end | `Ich [kann/muss/möchte] ... [Infinitiv].` | P01 | `Ich kann sprechen Deutsch` |
| P12 | Separable verbs | `Ich [stehe] um 7 [auf].` | P01, P11 | `Ich aufstehe` |
| P13 | Dativ in fixed phrases | `mit dem Bus`, `zur Uni`, `beim Arzt` | P07 | `mit der Bus` |
| P14 | Perfekt with `haben` | `Ich habe [gestern] ... gemacht.` | P03, P11 | participle not at end |
| P15 | Perfekt with `sein` | `Ich bin nach Graz gefahren.` | P14 | `Ich habe gefahren` |
| P16 | `und / aber / denn` (no change) | `..., aber ich lerne noch.` | P01 | changing word order after *denn* |
| P17 | `weil / dass / wenn` (verb last) | `..., weil ich müde bin.` | P16 | `weil ich bin müde` (heard in speech, wrong in writing) |
| P18 | `deshalb / trotzdem` (verb next) | `Deshalb lerne ich.` | P16 | `Deshalb ich lerne` |
| P19 | Polite requests | `Könnten Sie ...?` `Ich würde gern ...` | P11 | `Können Sie` is fine; `Könnten` is softer |
| P20 | Relative clauses, passive | later | A2 functioning in speech | |

Recitation (Pahara) tables: `sein`, `haben`, `können/müssen/möchten`, personal pronouns, `der/die/das → den/die/das`, W-words. Each recitation is followed by a speaking task.

## 12-week sequence

| Week | Patterns | Decks / scenarios | Bahar Mission examples | Ladder target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P01, P02, P07 | Greetings, politeness, repair phrases | Say *Guten Morgen* / *Hallo* to 3 people. *Danke, schönen Tag noch.* | 1-turn |
| 2 | P04, P05 | Canteen, café, seat questions | *Ist hier frei?* · *Mahlzeit* (recognise, answer) | 1-turn |
| 3 | P03, P06, P08 | Supermarket, Kassa, Sackerl, numbers, prices | Answer *Brauchen Sie ein Sackerl?* · *Mit Karte, bitte.* | 2-turn |
| 4 | P09, P10 | Ordering coffee/food | Order a Melange fully in German | 2-turn |
| 5 | P11 | Transport, directions, Öffis | Ask *Fährt die U1 zum Stephansplatz?* | 2-turn |
| 6 | P12 | Daily routine, university timetable | Tell a classmate when your lecture starts | 4-turn |
| 7 | P13 | Dorm, repair, parcel, pharmacy | Report a repair problem in 2 sentences | 4-turn |
| 8 | review + checkpoint | Self-introduction 30 s | Introduce yourself to one classmate | 30–90 s (2/week) |
| 9 | P14 | What I did at the weekend | Answer *Was hast du am Wochenende gemacht?* | 30–90 s |
| 10 | P15, P16 | Plans, travel, weather small talk | Make a simple plan with a classmate | 30–90 s |
| 11 | P17, P19 | Polite requests, appointments, barber | Book or change an appointment by phone or in person | 30–90 s (3/week) |
| 12 | P18 + A1 functional check | All scenarios mixed | 2-minute conversation without switching to English | 2+ min |

Rules:
- A week's patterns are not "done" until production accuracy on recent drills is ≥ 85 %. If not, repeat the week with new contexts rather than moving on.
- Recovery phrases (*Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?* etc.) are introduced in week 1 and stay in Manzil all 12 weeks.
- Culture cards follow the deck labels: `use now` cards enter intake with their scenario week; `recognise now` cards enter as recognition-only; `later` cards stay out of intake.

## Weeks 13 to 36

Phrase decks: `5_smalltalk_a2.json` (weeks 13 to 24) and `6_b1_expression.json` (weeks 25 to 36) flow into the daily Sabaq after the A1 decks. Conversations live in `data/conversations/{a1,a2,b1}.json`. The app recommends the next conversation whose week is at most one week ahead of the furthest phrase Danish has started.

| Weeks | Level | Focus | Conversations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 to 15 | A2 | Weekend stories, why Vienna, food from home | a2-wochenende, a2-warum-wien, a2-essen-heimat |
| 16 to 18 | A2 | Plans and times, doctor by phone, viewing a WG room | a2-plaene-samstag, a2-arzttermin, a2-wg-zimmer |
| 19 to 21 | A2 | Work background, Christmas market, U-Bahn disruption | a2-beruf-erfahrung, a2-weihnachtsmarkt, a2-ubahn-stoerung |
| 22 to 24 | A2 | Group work, complaints, explaining your culture. A2 functional check | a2-gruppenarbeit, a2-paket-reklamation, a2-cricket-erklaeren |
| 25 to 27 | B1 | Telling stories, opinions about Vienna | b1-missverstaendnis, b1-meinung-wien |
| 28 to 30 | B1 | Job interview, comparing countries, polite requests | b1-vorstellungsgespraech, b1-vergleich-heimat, b1-vermieter-bitte |
| 31 to 33 | B1 | Discussion, dialect recognition | b1-ki-diskussion, b1-heuriger |
| 34 to 36 | B1 | Exam format: plan together. B1 mock | b1-gemeinsam-planen |

Practice order for each conversation: Listen → Roleplay (own lines hidden) → Your words (only the English idea shown) → use one line with a real person (Bahar Mission). Two roleplays mark it as practised.

Realistic pace: the Goethe-Institut estimates 260 to 490 hours of instruction from zero to B1. The week numbers are an order, not a calendar promise; FSRS review load sets the real speed.

## In the app (v2.4)

The curriculum above is now data, not only this document:

- `data/curriculum.json`: 36 weeks with title, can-do goals and grammar codes. Shown in the **Kurs** tab.
- `data/grammar/{a1,a1b,a2,a2b,b1,b1b}.json`: 55 grammar lessons (P01–P55). Each has an explanation, a slot diagram or table, examples (with audio), a common mistake and 8 to 10 drills (fill the gap, build the sentence from tiles, transform). `extra_drills.json` adds 5 drills to each of P01–P42 by code. Drill order is shuffled every round. Mastery = 85 % over the last 4 practice rounds. The daily **Qaida** step picks the earliest unmastered pattern up to the current week.
- `data/seed/7_words.json`: about 460 core words in 26 topics (gender, plural or key verb forms), introduced through the daily Sabaq by week.
- `data/conversations/`: 46 conversations (A1 16, A2 18, B1 12).

Extra patterns added beyond the table above: P29 dative pronouns · P30 two-way prepositions · P31 possessives · P32 nicht · P33 imperative · P34 um/am/im · P35 adjective endings · P36 future plans · P37 passive · P38 Perfekt of separable/inseparable verbs · P39 Präteritum in stories · P40 genitive, wegen, trotz · P41 bevor/nachdem/während/seit · P42 verbs with prepositions · P43 plurals · P44 es gibt · P45 gern/lieber/mögen · P46 für/ohne/durch/gegen/um · P47 telling the time · P48 numbers and prices · P49 object pronouns · P50 TeKaMoLo word order · P51 welcher / was für ein · P52 seit/vor/für · P53 Plusquamperfekt · P54 je … desto · P55 lassen.

`worker/course.test.ts` checks that every week has content, every grammar code in the curriculum exists, and every drill answer is valid.

Generated practice (`worker/workout.ts`), same drill screen:

- **Word workout** (`/workout/words/:week`, week 0 = all words so far): 12 words, each once, as der/die/das, plural choice, meaning both ways, Perfekt with hat/ist, or typing the German. Wrong options come from the same week. Stored as `W<week>` in grammar_practice. Shown on the week page and as the daily **Lughat** step.
- **Grammar mix** (`/workout/grammar`): 10 drills from lessons up to the current week: shaky lessons first, then mastered ones practised longest ago, then untried ones. Results are stored per lesson, so they count toward each lesson's mastery.
